## Users
Two audiences, weighted equally:
- Recruiters and hiring managers screening full-stack candidates. They skim: who is this, what has he shipped, where is the resume.
- People in the Frappe / ERPNext ecosystem (maintainers, consultancies, clients) who judge by merged upstream work and ERP integration experience.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Harsh Patadia, Full-Stack Developer at Resilient Tech. Success: a visitor leaves knowing what he builds, sees proof (shipped projects, merged OSS PRs), and can reach GitHub, LinkedIn, email or the resume in one click.
